## Purpose

1. full schema-valid publication dates;
2. edition-specific evidence for the existing Singer Evidence Contribution proposals.

## 2016 Kindle edition

Accepted D11 identity:

`neurodiversity-source-singer-2016-kindle`

Evidence routes:

- Judy Singer's publications page: `https://neurodiversity2.blogspot.com/p/blog-page.html`
- Judy Singer's citation bibliography: `https://neurodiversity2.blogspot.com/p/bibliography-how-to-cite-my-work.html`
- Judy Singer's selected quotations: `https://neurodiversity2.blogspot.com/p/selected-quotations.html`
- Goodreads edition catalogue: `https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/31671068-neurodiversity`

Findings:

- Singer's own pages identify the work as first published in 2016 and identify the Kindle edition.
- Singer describes the book as a later introduction plus a reprint of the 1998 thesis.
- Goodreads supplies the precise date `2016-07-03` for the 98-page Kindle edition.

Decision boundary:

`2016-07-03` remains **secondary-catalogue candidate data**, not verified enrichment. Singer's first-party material confirms the year and edition identity, but not the exact day in the evidence inspected here.

### Contribution binding

Claim 1 can move to owner review as an edition-specific `compatible` binding because Singer's own publication/quotation material ties the reproduced thesis to the autistic-community, neurological-diversity and movement material underlying the existing proposal.

Claim 2 remains weaker. The 2016 edition is documented as containing the thesis and a new-paradigm introduction, but direct edition-level text is preferred before accepting the more specific social-constructionist/medical-model elements of the existing Claim 2 proposal.

Decision boundary:

`2017-09-05` is a **corroborated catalogue candidate** and is mature enough for owner review, but is not made authoritative or silently installed by this research pass.

### Contribution binding

Both existing Singer contribution proposals can move to owner review for the 2017 edition as `compatible` bindings. The edition-level evidence independently identifies the content underlying both proposals.

## Important asymmetry

The research deliberately does **not** assign the same confidence to every field merely because the editions share the original thesis:

- 2017 full date: stronger catalogue corroboration;
- 2016 full date: weaker day-level evidence;
- 2017 Claim 1 + Claim 2: owner-review candidates;
- 2016 Claim 1: owner-review candidate;
- 2016 Claim 2: direct text still preferred.

This preserves D11's rule that edition identity does not imply automatic Claim-support duplication.
