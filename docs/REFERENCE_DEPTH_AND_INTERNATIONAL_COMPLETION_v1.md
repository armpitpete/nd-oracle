# ND Oracle reference-depth and international completion v1

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: implementation candidate

## Purpose

Complete the bounded backlog for Books & media, Sleep, Food & eating, Mobility & travel, the deliberately deferred Republic-of-Ireland work, and the three-country international architecture proof without weakening ND Oracle's evidence, clinical, jurisdiction, privacy or release boundaries.

Completion here means **reference-complete for the declared governed remit**, not exhaustive worldwide coverage.

## Completion decisions

### Books & media
Complete when the catalogue has:
- broader neurodivergent authorship beyond autism/ADHD;
- at least one governed podcast route and one documentary/media route;
- accessible-format inspection;
- an introductory reading route that does not create a hidden recommendation ranking;
- permanent lived-experience versus clinical-evidence separation;
- no important newly added Resource without a Question route.

### Sleep
Complete when:
- persistent insomnia navigation is represented across England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland;
- children/young-person sleep has an explicit route;
- sensory/environment and shift-work routes remain;
- medication/melatonin remains information-only with hard start/stop/increase/decrease refusal;
- suspected other sleep disorders are handed to clinical assessment rather than diagnosed by ND Oracle;
- circadian/specialist-service gaps remain explicit where national first-party routing is not uniform.

### Food & eating
Complete when:
- ARFID information is separate from self-diagnosis;
- Scotland and Wales first-party ARFID routes supplement the existing NHS England route;
- swallowing difficulty is explicitly separated from sensory food preference and ARFID;
- child/parent navigation and dietetic/SLT handoff are visible;
- pica remains a clinical-sign/symptom issue rather than a sensory preference label;
- acute choking/swallowing concerns retain urgent human-service routing.

### Mobility & travel
Complete when:
- bus/coach statutory assistance and accessible-information rules are represented for Great Britain;
- Scotland concessionary travel remains distinct;
- Northern Ireland operator assistance is not collapsed into GB rules;
- journey-planning/sensory/disruption routes are present;
- operator metadata remains descriptive, never a quality score;
- local/operator arrangements never become national promises.

### Republic of Ireland
Completion of the deferred v1 follow-on means:
- child ADHD source-readiness has been rechecked;
- the absence of a sufficiently stable national access route remains explicit if still true;
- regional/implementation variation is governed rather than hidden;
- private-assessment navigation is bounded without provider ranking;
- broader Ireland domains are selected only from demonstrated source-readiness and user value.

Current 2026-09-05 decision: **child ADHD remains deliberately deferred**. The current HSE CAMHS operational framework does not by itself establish a uniform national paediatric ADHD assessment/access route. ND Oracle must not manufacture one.

### International architecture
The architecture proof requires three real non-UK packages before schema reconsideration:
1. Republic of Ireland — already implemented;
2. Australia — bounded Assessment & diagnosis package;
3. Canada — bounded Assessment & diagnosis package using Canada-wide orientation plus explicit provincial implementation example.

After the third package, perform an observed-evidence architecture review. A core geography schema change remains **not justified** unless the implemented packages expose repeated fields that sidecars cannot safely maintain.

## Protected boundaries

This candidate does not authorize:
- merge;
- deployment;
- DNS or Cloudflare changes;
- secrets;
- provider ranking;
- diagnosis or medication authority;
- automatic geolocation;
- personalised ranking;
- mass-country generation.

Exact-head validation, hostile review, protected merge and protected deployment remain separate gates.
