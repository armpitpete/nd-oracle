## Reading rule

`national` means suitable for a Republic-of-Ireland national route within the source's stated scope. `regional/local` evidence must not be promoted into a national rule. A clinical model or protocol does not by itself prove current service availability.

## Cross-border interpretation

1. Republic-of-Ireland HSE evidence never creates a Northern Ireland HSC/NHS rule.
2. Northern Ireland HSC/NHS evidence never creates a Republic-of-Ireland HSE rule.
3. The short label `Ireland` is used only where the Republic scope is explicit and unambiguous.
4. England Right to Choose is not a Republic-of-Ireland entitlement.
5. A national protocol/model describes standards or intended configuration; current local access still requires implementation evidence where material.
