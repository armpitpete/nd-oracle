## Games & downtime acceptance boundary

The section now has 13 governed game Resources and 12 Games & downtime Questions, plus supporting governed routes for Steam's developer-declared accessibility-feature system and one geographically restricted adult autistic gaming group. Coverage is driven by observable facets such as timing, reaction demand, failure consequences, interruption, input complexity, social load and mode dependence. Steam user tags such as `Relaxing` and `Cozy` remain discovery signals only, and Steam accessibility declarations remain inspection metadata rather than certification. `low-sensory-demand-games` stays `not_currently_answerable` until sufficiently comparable per-game accessibility evidence exists.

## Editorial rule

A gap is not filled by adding a page alone. A useful fill needs a governed Question, a reviewed Resource with access/scope/limitations, a Concept where explanation is needed, or governed Evidence for a serious efficacy, safety, legal or diagnostic proposition. Questions remain the default expansion driver.
