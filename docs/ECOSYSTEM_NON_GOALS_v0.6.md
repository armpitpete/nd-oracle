# Ecosystem v0.6 non-goals

This milestone does not attempt to solve discovery at scale. It proves that diverse Resources can be governed and published safely.

It specifically does not attempt:

- personalised matching;
- medical triage;
- “best for ADHD/autism” rankings;
- affiliate commerce;
- user reviews;
- community moderation;
- dynamic faceted search;
- AI summarisation of third-party pages;
- completeness claims;
- automated trust scores.

Those capabilities require separate evidence, privacy, moderation, accessibility and product review rather than being inferred from the existence of a Resource catalogue.
