# ND Oracle content-gap map v0.9

Date: 2026-08-28

## Purpose

Content expansion is driven from ordinary needs rather than from diagnosis labels. This matrix is the editorial backlog map: a domain can have some governed content and still remain incomplete. `Covered` means the current corpus gives a useful starting route; it does not mean the domain is exhausted or universally applicable.

## Editorial rule

A gap is not filled merely by adding a page. A useful fill normally needs at least one of:

1. a governed practical Question that states what is and is not currently answerable;
2. a reviewed Resource with a working access route, scope and limitations;
3. a reviewed Concept when the reader needs an explanatory idea rather than a directory entry;
4. governed evidence when a serious efficacy, safety, legal or diagnostic proposition is being made.

Questions remain the default expansion driver: a useful unanswered Question exposes the missing Concepts, Resources or evidence more reliably than expanding a diagnosis taxonomy for its own sake.
