# Ecosystem v0.6 content rules

- Prefer current first-party identity/access information for whether a Resource exists and how to reach it.
- Do not use first-party efficacy marketing as independent evidence.
- Record obvious poor-fit conditions, not only benefits.
- Keep commercial ownership and advocacy position visible.
- Do not infer suitability from diagnosis labels.
- Keep geographical scope explicit for services and law/policy resources.
- Review time-sensitive service/resource entries more often than stable conceptual history.
- Remove active presentation when closure or withdrawal is established.
