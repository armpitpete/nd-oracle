# Assessment & Diagnosis — UK source matrix v1

Reviewed: 2026-09-03

## Reading rule

`national` means the source is suitable for a nation-level public route within its stated scope. `local` means it is evidence about one health board, Trust, ICB or provider pathway and must not be generalised to the whole nation. `clinical-guideline` describes professional assessment standards and does not by itself establish a local access route.

## Cross-cutting interpretation

1. Right to Choose is represented only for England.
2. NICE guidance is used to describe clinical assessment standards within its applicable scope, not to manufacture a public referral route.
3. Scottish NHS inform's explicit health-board variation is preserved.
4. Wales adult ADHD is represented as locally variable while the national programme develops more consistent options.
5. Northern Ireland adult ADHD is represented as uneven/partially commissioned based on the 2026 Department of Health assessment, not as a single stable national service.
6. Private-assessment acceptance is always a check-before-paying question, not an ND Oracle guarantee.
7. Child support and educational support are not made conditional on a diagnosis unless the specific governed source says that a particular service requires one.
