## Disposition notes

- Morphological decomposition is used only where it is a safe learning aid.
- Acronyms are expanded as words.
- Technical phrases are split into meaningful phrase components.
- Single-chunk entries are deliberate where forced etymology would be misleading.
- PDA carries an explicit contested-terminology boundary.
- Comorbidity explains the medical wording and points readers toward the plainer co-occurrence concept when disease framing is unnecessary.

## Excluded after audit

Common or sufficiently self-explanatory expressions such as reasonable adjustments, safeguarding and lived experience remain ordinary prose in v1. They can be promoted later if owner or human testing demonstrates comprehension problems.


## Generated-site first-use audit

The current candidate build produces:

- **175** Topic, Question or Resource reading pages with at least one terminology guide;
- **186** grouped first-use terminology components;
- **223** in-page term explanations;
- **33** of the 52 canonical entries used inline under the first-meaningful-use threshold;
- **19** entries retained glossary-only because their current occurrences are incidental, one-off, inside deeper evidence/provenance detail, or outside the eligible reading-page surfaces;
- maximum **2** terminology components on any one page;
- maximum **4** explained terms on any one page.

The density limit is deliberate. A term qualifies for automatic in-page help when it is central to the title/lede or occurs in at least two eligible prose paragraphs. This keeps incidental mentions from producing large terminology walls while preserving the canonical glossary entry for every audited term.
