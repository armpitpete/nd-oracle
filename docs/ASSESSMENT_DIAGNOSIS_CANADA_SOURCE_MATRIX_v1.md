## Federal/provincial interpretation

1. Federal health information may orient a Canada-wide Question but cannot create provincial service entitlement.
2. Ontario diagnostic hubs are not Canadian national services.
3. Provincial eligibility, waiting, funding and programme rules remain provincial.
4. A future province/territory must enter only through a useful bounded route with its own first-party evidence.
5. ND Oracle does not diagnose or rank providers.

## Source language and freshness

The reviewed package sources are English-language government pages. Future material must preserve the original source language and material translation uncertainty. Federal orientation can use the normal authoritative-guidance cadence; provincial implementation should be reviewed more frequently when programme arrangements change.
