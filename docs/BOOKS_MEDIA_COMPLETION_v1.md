# Books & media completion v1

Date: 2026-09-05

## Acceptance boundary

Books & media is reference-complete when readers can distinguish:
- lived experience;
- practical perspective;
- fiction/representation;
- introductory neurodiversity material;
- podcast/documentary media;
- clinical/research evidence.

Popularity, awards, sales, publisher description or personal recognition do not establish clinical truth or efficacy.

## Accessible formats

Where a publisher explicitly exposes ebook/audiobook formats, ND Oracle records that as access metadata. Availability, territorial rights, price and platform can change and are not guaranteed.

## Permanent boundaries

- Memoir is evidence of the author's experience, not prevalence or universal causation.
- A documentary is not a diagnostic instrument.
- A podcast host or guest is not clinical authority merely by being featured.
- Fiction is not therapy.
- ND Oracle does not rank one book/media item as universally best.
