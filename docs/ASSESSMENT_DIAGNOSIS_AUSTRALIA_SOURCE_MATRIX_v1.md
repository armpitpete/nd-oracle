## Scope rule

1. Australia-wide information can orient a national Question.
2. A future state/territory route must be explicitly narrower.
3. State/territory prescribing or service rules must not be generalized nationally.
4. National orientation does not imply publicly funded assessment availability everywhere.
5. Provider identity does not become provider endorsement.

## Source language and freshness

The reviewed source pages are English-language first-party public-health material. Preserve source language metadata if non-English authoritative material is added later. Review national guidance on the authoritative-guidance cadence; review volatile state/territory implementation on a shorter cadence.
