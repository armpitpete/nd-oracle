# Relationships & family — UK reference contract v1

Date: 2026-09-03
Base: `8679d6ebd555b46b6ba42950276b2d1722d1737b`

## Purpose

Define a bounded, neurodiversity-aware Relationships & family reference slice that helps people find governed information about friendship, partners, family communication, boundaries, conflict, parenting and service access without turning ND Oracle into a relationship adjudicator, therapist, diagnostic service or safeguarding decision-maker.

## Core boundary

Question routes remain **Relevant to inspect, not recommended**.

ND Oracle may help a reader:

- name a communication, sensory, processing-time or executive-function need;
- find practical first-party or established specialist information;
- prepare a conversation or request for an adjustment;
- distinguish support for a neurodivergent parent from support for parenting a neurodivergent child;
- find official safeguarding or domestic-abuse routes when the user's wording calls for them;
- identify jurisdiction-specific disability-access information for services.

ND Oracle must not:

- tell a person whether to start, continue or end a relationship;
- decide who is right in a disagreement;
- infer that a partner, parent, child or friend is autistic/ADHD from behaviour described in a query;
- excuse coercion, violence, sexual pressure or other harmful conduct as neurodivergence;
- present one communication or relationship style as the normal standard;
- recommend a named therapist, counsellor or private service as clinically suitable;
- give personalised legal conclusions;
- replace emergency, safeguarding, social-care, clinical or legal judgement.

## Relationship model

The section treats communication difficulty as potentially interactional rather than automatically locating the problem in the neurodivergent person. Useful routes should support explicit communication, processing time, sensory boundaries, clarification, repair and mutually understood expectations without requiring masking or performance of neurotypical social conventions.

## Route families

### Friendships

Cover making and maintaining friendships, misunderstandings, contact expectations, direct communication, social energy and repair after miscommunication. A friendship route must not assume that more social contact is always the desired outcome.

### Partners and close relationships

Cover communication preferences, processing time, routines/change, shared-space sensory needs and difficult conversations. The section does not decide relationship compatibility.

### Boundaries and conflict

Cover expressing limits, requesting time/space, written follow-up, clarification and de-escalation accommodations. Ordinary conflict material must remain separate from abuse and coercive-control routes.

### Intimacy and consent

Cover explicit communication about touch, sensory preferences and consent at a bounded informational level. Consent remains necessary regardless of diagnosis or communication style. Sexual-health or clinical questions route to appropriate external health information rather than becoming personalised advice here.

### Neurodivergent and disabled parents

Cover the parent's own accessibility, sensory, executive-function and service-communication needs. This route family is distinct from guidance for a parent/carer supporting a neurodivergent child.

### Parenting neurodivergent children

Cover needs-led support, communication, sensory environment, routines/transitions and coordination with health/education services. Support must not be framed as requiring a diagnosis where the relevant support route does not require one.

### Wider family and carers

Use shared family-communication routes when they generalise. Create separate sibling, kinship-carer or unpaid-carer routes only when a distinct user problem or service system justifies them.

## Safeguarding boundary

Potential abuse, coercive control, sexual violence or immediate-danger wording must never be answered only with ordinary relationship-communication material. The public corpus may list official help routes and explain their scope but must not determine from a short query whether abuse has occurred.

## Jurisdiction model

General friendship, communication and parenting information may be UK-wide or non-jurisdictional when the source and wording genuinely support that scope.

Disability discrimination and service-access routes must preserve the legal split:

- England, Scotland and Wales: Equality Act 2010 / EHRC service-access material;
- Northern Ireland: separate disability-discrimination law and Equality Commission / nidirect material.

A Great Britain route must not surface as a Northern Ireland legal entitlement, and a Northern Ireland route must not be presented as Great Britain law.

## Source hierarchy

Prefer, in order appropriate to the claim or practical route:

1. government, NHS/HSC, equality regulator or statutory body;
2. established national specialist organisation with clear authorship and scope;
3. professional body or regulated service information;
4. peer-reviewed evidence where ND Oracle makes a serious testable proposition not adequately supported by operational sources;
5. lived-experience material as lived experience, not as general authority.

A Resource listing is not an endorsement. Relationship programmes, counselling and private providers remain claimless unless a separately governed evidence claim is justified.

## Language and inclusion

Do not assume heterosexuality, cisgender identity, monogamy, marriage, biological parenthood or a nuclear family. Use partner, friend, family member, parent/carer and child/young person where the distinction matters. Do not treat directness, eye contact, small talk or frequency of contact as universal measures of caring.

## Acceptance gates

The candidate can be called complete for v1 only when:

- friendship misunderstanding no longer falls to `no_match`;
- parenting a neurodivergent child no longer resolves to support for an autistic parent;
- separate governed journeys exist for friendships, partners, boundaries/conflict, parenting a neurodivergent child and disabled/neurodivergent parent access;
- a bounded intimacy/consent route exists without personalised sexual or relationship advice;
- official safeguarding/domestic-abuse information is reachable without implying that ND Oracle has determined abuse;
- GB and Northern Ireland disability-access routes are jurisdiction-contained;
- every new Resource has a typed HTTPS locator, limitations and review state;
- no new claim-bearing proposition is introduced without governed evidence;
- no ranking-weight, clinical-boundary, privacy/query-storage, analytics or AI-authority weakening occurs;
- deterministic Python/browser discovery parity remains intact;
- full schema, provenance, freshness, evidence, discovery and public compatibility regression passes;
- hostile diff review passes;
- the exact candidate SHA is separately authorised before merge;
- post-merge production deployment remains a separately authorised exact-SHA action.

## Non-goals

- no relationship compatibility scoring;
- no diagnosis inference about relatives or partners;
- no provider ranking;
- no personalised therapy recommendation;
- no family-law adjudication;
- no personalised safeguarding determination;
- no graph database or new schema solely for this section;
- no query storage, analytics, profiling or external search in public discovery;
- no production deployment in this candidate without separate authority.
