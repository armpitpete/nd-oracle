| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Other sleep disorders | NHS inform | Scotland | https://www.nhsinform.scot/illnesses-and-conditions/lungs-and-airways/obstructive-sleep-apnoea/ | Demonstrates specialist investigation for suspected sleep disorder; not a general national sleep-clinic entitlement. |

## Boundary

The source set supports insomnia navigation and clinical handoff. It does **not** support ND Oracle diagnosing insomnia, circadian-rhythm disorder, sleep apnoea or another sleep disorder, nor choosing medication.
