# ND Oracle Organisations & peer community UK reference slice v1

Date: 2026-09-04

## Purpose

This contract turns Organisations & peer community from a good bounded seed into a strong UK reference seed without turning ND Oracle into a provider-rating service, a universal local directory, a safety-certification system or a substitute for clinical, crisis or professional support.

## Taxonomy

The slice distinguishes:

- **organisation** — a formal body with its own governance;
- **peer-controlled / user-led organisation** — formal decision control by people represented, where that control is explicitly evidenced;
- **lived-experience volunteer-led group** — activities organised by people with lived experience without claiming formal organisation-wide control;
- **support group / community** — a place or programme for connection or mutual support;
- **directory** — a discovery surface containing independent listings;
- **professional/service provider** — a service route that must not be relabelled as peer-led merely because lived experience informs it;
- **informal online space** — a community whose governance may be limited or unclear.

"Peer-led", "user-led" and "DPO" are descriptive governance properties, not quality, efficacy, accessibility or safety certifications.

## Geographic contract

Coverage preserves England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland explicitly. A UK-based charity is not assumed to have four-nation local provision.

The slice includes bounded geographic proofs for York/Yorkshire, Cumbria, Highland, Powys and Fermanagh. These proofs demonstrate that the discovery model can reach regional or rural starting points; they do not claim complete local coverage.

ND Oracle deliberately does not attempt to catalogue every local group. It records maintained directory and search routes and a governed "no suitable local group" path so absence from one catalogue never becomes a fabricated recommendation.

## Cross-neurodivergent boundary

No independently evidenced, complete, peer-governed UK-wide cross-neurodivergent directory was found in this slice.

The candidate therefore combines:

- condition-specific networks for autism, ADHD, dyslexia, dyspraxia/DCD and Tourette syndrome;
- user-led/self-advocacy networks;
- Disabled People's Organisation membership routes;
- one commercially operated cross-neurodivergent event directory with its conflict of interest made explicit.

This is deliberate. ND Oracle must not manufacture a false unified directory merely to make the section look complete.

## Accessibility metadata

Machine-readable metadata in `contracts/organisations-peer-community-uk-v1.json` records only published or inspectable facts and uses explicit uncertainty states.

Relevant variables include:

- online / in-person / hybrid;
- asynchronous text;
- speaking, camera or video requirements where published;
- group size or capacity where published;
- third-party platform dependency;
- registration and cost;
- sensory or venue access where published.

"Not found" means the reviewed public source did not establish the fact. It does not mean an adjustment or feature is absent.

## Moderation and safeguarding metadata

For communities, inspect:

- whether moderation or facilitation is described;
- reporting and rule information where public;
- age and audience boundaries;
- medical-advice boundaries;
- public versus member-only participation.

The contract contains no safety score. Published rules are useful inspection data but do not prove that moderation is effective.

## Privacy metadata

The section distinguishes public directories, publicly readable forums, members-only or platform spaces and registration-based meetings. "Private group" is never represented as a confidentiality guarantee.

## Evidence boundary

The section records one directly relevant peer-reviewed UK qualitative study as **contextual, not claim-bearing** evidence. It supports the design choice to expose accessibility, facilitator-support, sustainability and specialist-service boundaries, but it is not used to claim that peer support works for every autistic person, still less every neurodivergent person.

A normalized Evidence/Claim expansion is intentionally not forced into this content slice because the frozen Evidence Layer remains a separate governed contract.

## Safety and authority boundaries

ND Oracle does not:

- declare a group safe;
- endorse a listed organisation;
- infer accessibility or peer leadership;
- convert charity status into quality;
- treat peer posts as clinical, legal or financial authority;
- send a person in crisis to ordinary peer support instead of crisis or human services;
- generalise England-only routes to the UK;
- convert unknown metadata into negative claims.

## Acceptance threshold

The section reaches **Strong UK reference seed** when:

1. all four nations have meaningful bounded discovery routes;
2. the resource corpus is no longer autism and ADHD only;
3. several explicitly user-led or peer-controlled organisations are represented;
4. cross-ND routes exist with completeness and conflict limits visible;
5. accessibility, moderation and privacy metadata use tested uncertainty semantics;
6. representative discovery and hostile cases pass;
7. frozen clinical, jurisdiction, privacy, provenance, ranking, Evidence-Layer and production-state boundaries remain unchanged;
8. full exact-head CI and hostile diff review pass.

Residual gaps after v1 are expected to include DLD and dyscalculia-specific peer networks, deeper local accessibility publication, independent moderation or outcome evidence and ongoing directory freshness.
