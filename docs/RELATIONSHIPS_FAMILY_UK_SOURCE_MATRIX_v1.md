# Relationships & family — UK source matrix v1

Date: 2026-09-03

## Purpose

Record the external source baseline for the bounded Relationships & family UK v1 candidate. Resource inclusion is navigational, not endorsement. Serious efficacy, safety, diagnostic and legal propositions remain evidence-governed separately.

| Source | Scope used by ND Oracle | Why included | Important limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| National Autistic Society — Making friends, autistic adults | Friendship information for autistic adults | Existing gap includes friendship misunderstandings and maintenance | Last reviewed 2020; autism-specific; some social-convention advice may not suit every reader |
| National Autistic Society — Family relationships | Partners/family communication and perspectives | Relationship-specific support route with multiple family audiences | Some material is written from relative/partner viewpoints; not universal authority |
| NHS — Supporting an autistic child | Parenting an autistic child, communication, sensory environment, practical support | Current first-party UK health-information starting point | Local education/service steps vary; autism-specific |
| Contact — parent/carer helpline | UK parent/carer human advice route for disabled children up to 25 | Separates parenting a disabled/neurodivergent child from support for a neurodivergent parent | Charity advice, not emergency/clinical/legal representation |
| GOV.UK — Domestic abuse: how to get help | Official safeguarding/domestic-abuse starting point and current support routes | Prevents ordinary communication material being the only response to fear/control/violence/sexual pressure | ND Oracle cannot determine whether abuse occurred; nation-specific services/remedies vary |
| NHS inform — Healthy relationships + Devon Sexual Health NHS — Consent | Consent, boundaries and sexual-health communication starting points | Bounded intimacy/consent coverage without inventing ND-specific sexual advice | Public-health sources from different UK nations; not one harmonised legal guide |
| EHRC reasonable-adjustments code + Equality Commission NI disability guidance | Disability access to services, preserving Great Britain / Northern Ireland split | Disabled/neurodivergent parent service-access starting point | Legal application depends on facts; ND Oracle does not decide disability status or reasonableness |
| Leicestershire Partnership NHS Trust — autism and communication | Written/AAC/alternative communication and neuroaffirmative communication support | Useful when spoken relationship or family communication is inaccessible | Local service references are not national entitlements |

## Source decisions

1. No relationship programme or therapist is represented as effective or suitable.
2. No private provider ranking is introduced.
3. Friendship and family guidance from specialist organisations is retained as guidance with limitations, not as evidence that one social style is correct.
4. Consent and domestic-abuse routes are deliberately separated from ordinary relationship communication.
5. Great Britain and Northern Ireland disability-access frameworks are presented together only as a jurisdiction signpost; ND Oracle does not collapse them into one law.
6. Parent/carer support for a neurodivergent child is kept separate from support for a parent who is themselves neurodivergent or disabled.
