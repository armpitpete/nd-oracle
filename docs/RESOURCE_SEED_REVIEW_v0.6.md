# Initial ecosystem resource review v0.6

Review date: 2026-08-28

This record explains the first public Resource seed. It is not a ranked recommendation list.

## Included seed

The candidate contains 15 reviewed Resource objects across tools/apps, games, services, organisations and a book. Selection was deliberately varied to test the public model rather than maximise catalogue size.

## Review method

For each Resource the official current surface was checked for identity, purpose, availability/access route and material limitations that could be represented without turning first-party marketing into evidence.

The seed uses empty `claims` arrays where the listing is descriptive only. This is intentional: ND Oracle can say that a product, game, service, organisation or book exists and describe its declared function without claiming that it is effective for a diagnosis or individual.

Commercial products retain commercial-conflict notes. Charities and advocacy organisations retain their organisational position and self-interest rather than being treated as neutral merely because they are nonprofits.

## Stale-resource rejection

ADHD Foundation was considered but rejected as an active current support listing after its official website was found to state that it ceased trading on 30 August 2025 and was entering insolvency/liquidation.

This is a model acceptance case: ND Oracle should prefer a smaller current catalogue to a larger stale directory. Historical organisations can be represented later when historical context is itself useful, but must not appear as active support options.

## What this seed does not prove

The seed does not prove:

- completeness of any category;
- clinical efficacy;
- safety for every user;
- ND suitability merely because a product is marketed that way;
- community consensus;
- comparative superiority over omitted resources.

The next content expansion should add breadth only when review quality and freshness can be maintained.
